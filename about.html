The Story of My Hometown - Kg Laut, Kelantan

  • My hometown, Kg. Laut in Kelantan, It is a unique and beautiful mosque in terms of its architecture. The uniqueness of this mosque is that its structure is made of wood and does not use nails where it is only nailed.
  • The building is still intact and used as a place of worship. Now the mosque is located in Nilam Puri where it was moved in 1965 from the original site because it was found that the original site was in the Kelantan River.
  • Kampung Laut mosque is the oldest mosque still in existence in Kelantan and among the oldest in the archipelago. The mosque is said to have been built in the 18th century by Muslim expatriates who commuted between Champa and Java with the help of the locals. The original site of the mosque is located in Kampung Laut, Tumpat on the banks of the Kelantan River.
